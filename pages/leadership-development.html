Nexus Leadership Development Program

Develops confident, ethical, responsible student leaders.

About the Program

Developing The Leader Within You

What is it?

This program helps students build the confidence, knowledge and practical abilities required to lead responsibly in university, professional environments and communities.

Who is it for?

Students interested in developing leadership abilities, whether or not they hold a leadership position.

What do Students Gain?

Students gain practical leadership skills, confidence, and the ability to lead responsibly in university, professional environments and communities. More confident, responsible and capable leadership.

What We Cover

Leadership in action

Leadership Training

Developing leadership skills and confidence through workshops, mentorship and practical experiences.

Communication and public speaking

Enhancing communication skills for effective leadership and public speaking.

Ethical Leadership

Understanding ethical principles and applying them in leadership roles.

Student Leadership

Applying leadership skills in real student and community environments.

Team management

Learning to manage and lead teams effectively, including conflict resolution and collaboration.

Opportunities to lead Nexus initiatives

Students can take on leadership roles in Nexus programs, events and initiatives, applying their skills in real-world contexts.

Decision-making

Developing critical thinking and decision-making skills for effective leadership.

Problem-solving

Developing critical thinking and problem-solving skills for effective leadership.

Leadership challenges

Engaging in leadership challenges and projects to apply skills and gain practical experience.
