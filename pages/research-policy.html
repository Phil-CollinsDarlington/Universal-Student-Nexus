Nexus Research and Policy Program

Engages students in research and policy conversations that shape the future.

About the Program

Students thinking beyond today.

What is it?

This program creates a platform for students to explore issues affecting young people, education, communities and society while developing research, analytical and communication skills.

Who is it for?

Students interested in research, policy, analysis, social issues and analysis and evidence-based ideas.

What do Students Gain?

Students participate in research projects, policy discussions and knowledge-sharing activities that encourage critical thinking and evidence-based decision-making.
