Founder & Executive Director

Nassanga Laurine

Nassanga Laurine founded Universal Student Nexus with the ambition of bridging the gap between education and opportunity for students across Uganda and eventually the world.

From Leadership to Impact

My journey in leadership began through student representation, where I discovered the importance of listening, bringing people together, and turning students' needs into meaningful action. These experiences strengthened my passion for leadership, community building, and creating opportunities for others.

As I became more involved with students, I recognized a gap: many young people have the ambition and potential to succeed, but lack access to the right networks, mentorship, opportunities and practical guidance. This inspired me to create Universal Student Nexus (USN)—a community built around connecting students to the people, knowledge and opportunities that can help them grow.

Today, as the Founder & Executive Director of USN, I am focused on building an organization that empowers students not only to find opportunities, but to create them. My vision is to grow USN from its university beginnings into a wider network of students, institutions and partners committed to developing the next generation of leaders, innovators and changemakers.
