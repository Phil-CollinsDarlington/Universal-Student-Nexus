Nexus Mentorship Program

Connect. Learn. Grow.

About the Program

Guidance for the journey ahead

What is the Mentorship Program?

The Nexus Mentorship Program connects students with mentors who provide guidance, encouragement, practical knowledge and perspective as they navigate academic, personal and professional journeys.

Who is it for?

Students looking for guidance, accountability or professional perspective.

What Students Can Gain

Clearer goals, stronger networks and practical guidance

What We Cover

Areas of mentorship

One-on-one or group mentorship

Mentorship sessions with experienced mentors, either individually or in small groups.

Career and academic guidance

Advice and support for academic and career planning, including goal-setting.

Conversations with experienced professionals

Opportunities to learn from the experiences of professionals in various fields.

Peer mentorship opportunities

Mentorship from peers who have navigated similar academic or professional paths.

Guidance on university and early-career decisions

Support for making informed decisions about university choices, courses and early career paths.

Goal-setting and personal development

Mentorship focused on personal growth, skill development and achieving goals.
