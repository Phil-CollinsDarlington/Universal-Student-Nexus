Nexus Community Impact Program

Channels student energy and skill into meaningful local impact.

About the Program

Students creating meaningful impact

What is it?

This program gives students opportunities to use their time, skills and ideas to contribute to communities and address real-world social needs.

Why participate?

Students can develop practical experience while contributing to positive change in their communities.

Who is it for?

Students who want to make a difference in their communities and gain experience in social impact.

What Do Students Gain?

Students gain practical experience, leadership skills, and the satisfaction of contributing to meaningful community change.Civic responsibility plus practical teamwork, leadership and project experience.
